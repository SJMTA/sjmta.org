Masterclass Clinician: Michael Eisenberg

Michael Eisenberg is a versatile and acclaimed pianist, harpsichordist, and conductor whose captivating performances have resonated across five continents. His recent orchestral appearances include collaborations with the Pavarotti Orchestra, the Moravian Philharmonic Orchestra, the Stara Zagora State Orchestra, and the Orchestra Lirica di Milano, alongside chamber recitals with Bacchanalia.

A familiar face on the New York music scene, Michael was a featured artist on the Carnegie Hall Neighborhood Concert Series for seven years, showcasing chamber music of Renaissance and Baroque Italian composers. His talent was also recognized as a member of the Metropolitan Opera Guild roster, where he performed for the Placido Domingo Met Gala celebration. His artistry has reached a broad audience through broadcasts on National Public Radio, the BBC, CBC, and HBO.
