What we do

The South Jersey Music Teachers' Association is dedicated to promoting the professional interests of piano teachers and furthering the advancement of music education in the southern New Jersey area. South Jersey Music Teachers' Association offers a varied program of presentations, festivals, competitions, and performances, including:
  • Annual lecture series for teachers.
  • Performing opportunities for students.
  • Pedagogical resources and networking.

Join Us!

The South Jersey Music Teachers' Association is dedicated to promoting the professional interests of piano teachers and furthering the advancement of music education in the southern New Jersey area. Membership in SJMTA is your passport to enriching opportunities for you and for all of your piano students. Through our affiliation with MTNA, the Music Teachers National Association (www.mtna.org), the resources of a national network of music teachers is available to you as well. Want to check us out first? Our Tuesday Morning Speakers' Series is open to the public for a nominal fee of $5 per session. It's a great way to see what we have to offer, meet some of our members, and make contact with teachers who share your interests. Interested in joining us? Visit the MTNA website (www.mtna.org) and follow the directions for joining. Please use the form on our Contact Us page to ask a question or request more information.
